# CSMS V2 Agent Instructions

This project is a clean rebuild of the Central Store Management System.

## Non-negotiable business architecture

- Stock belongs to Store, not Office, Section, or User.
- Central Store is a Store belonging to Directorate; it is not an Office.
- Central Press is a Branch Office with its own Store.
- General Storekeeper and Assistant Storekeeper execute against the same Central Store.
- Deputy Superintendent, Stock & Stores controls Central Store approval/authorization workflows.
- Branch Head controls branch approval workflows.
- Branch Storekeeper executes branch stock transactions.
- Consumable category name is `CONSUMABLE`; do not use `MATERIAL` as the primary category.
- Petty Purchase is consumable-only; asset petty purchase is prohibited.
- Consumable stock identity is Store + Financial Year + Item.
- StockMovement is the authoritative consumable ledger.
- Never directly edit stock balances.
- Posted stock movements are immutable; corrections use compensating transactions.
- Requested quantity and actual issued quantity are separate.
- Zero and partial issues are valid.
- Central Store shortages close the requisition at the actual dispatched quantity; do not create an automatic pending balance.
- Supplier receipt stock is accepted quantity only.
- Assets have a continuous lifecycle and are not reset per financial year.
- Batch, expiry, reorder, low-stock, FIFO, LIFO, and FEFO controls are optional per Store + Item.
- Every stock-affecting operation must be atomic and concurrency-safe.
- Authorization is role + permission + scope + workflow state + business validation.

## Technical architecture

- FastAPI async endpoints for I/O-bound work.
- SQLAlchemy 2.x async ORM with `AsyncSession`.
- One request/task gets its own AsyncSession; never share an AsyncSession concurrently.
- PostgreSQL through asyncpg.
- Alembic for migrations.
- Routers should not contain business logic.
- Repositories should not decide business authorization.
- Services contain business rules.
- Stock posting must be centralized in the posting layer.
- Do not create separate stock-register/current-stock source-of-truth tables.

## Implementation style

- Prefer explicit, boring, maintainable code over clever abstractions.
- Add tests with each vertical slice.
- Review autogenerated migrations before applying them.
- Preserve auditability and historical data.
- Do not reintroduce assumptions from the old CSMS merely because the old code used them.

## Petty Purchase rules
- Petty Purchase is an acquisition/stock-entry record for consumables; procurement/purchase order management is outside CSMS.
- A Petty Purchase posts accepted purchased quantity as `PETTY_PURCHASE` stock IN.
- Immediate issue is optional and can only use an existing open manual indent linked to the petty purchase.
- When immediate issue is used, purchased IN and Issue OUT are posted atomically using the same posting_group_id.
- Immediate issue quantity may be partial and must not exceed purchased quantity, indent requested quantity, or stock available after the purchase.
- A zero immediate issue leaves the purchased quantity in stock and creates no ISSUE movement.
- Temporary petty-purchase items get a temporary Item identity flagged `is_temporary=true` so the stock ledger can reference a normal Item. They can later be promoted/renamed as permanent master items without rewriting history.
- Asset petty purchase is prohibited.
- Central Store petty purchase verification is performed by the Deputy Superintendent, Stock & Stores; branch-store petty purchase verification is performed by the Branch Head of that office.
- Storekeepers create and post petty purchases for their assigned stores after required verification.
